The Core Idea
John captures everything during the trip. Maria gets a structured report after. No chasing. No phone calls. No missing receipts.
John logs expense on road
        ↓
Receipt photo + amount captured
        ↓
Auto-categorized by type
        ↓
Trip completes
        ↓
Maria receives full trip report automatically

Expense Categories:

⛽ Fuel — amount, gallons, price per gallon, station name
🍔 Food / Meal — amount, receipt optional
🛣️ Toll — amount, location auto-filled from GPS
🅿️ Parking — amount, location auto-filled
🔧 Repair / Maintenance — amount, description, receipt required
🏨 Lodging — amount, receipt required
📦 Other — amount, description, receipt optional

Smart defaults:

Location auto-filled from GPS on every expense
Timestamp auto-filled
Trip ID auto-attached — no manual linking needed
